# The lobby with more tables than fit on one screen, of every game, and its names after a table. Make the
# player first with lab/manytables.py (4 tables of their own, 2 invitations, 18 tables waiting):
#   python3 test/lab/manytables.py nora 16501
# ln_lobby shows the first screenful and More, ln_more the rest and Back to the start, ln_back the first
# again. Then the player's Backgammon table is opened (its overlay loaded from the disk) and left:
# ln_after_table must show the same names as ln_lobby, and ln_newgame the games' names in the list of New
# game. Each name is a game's (Backgammon, Cannon Fire, ...), never stray characters.
wait 120
caps
title 1800
waittext ParlorNet: connect 1200
type P
waittext Name: 900
type nora\r
waittext Password 600
type Swordfish 1997\r
wait 1200
snap ln_lobby
click 205
wait 120
snap ln_more
click 205
wait 120
snap ln_back
click 0
wait 1500
snap ln_table
key esc
wait 1200
snap ln_after_table
click 205
wait 120
snap ln_after_more
click 200
wait 200
snap ln_newgame
key esc
wait 300
exit
