Rummy Tile help
Moving
Pick tiles with {RET}, then press {RET} on a set to put them there.

Keys for Rummy Tile
{OA}E ends your turn, {OA}U takes back a step and {OA}Z the whole turn.
{OA}M makes a new set, and {OA}B splits one. {OA}C says whether your turn would count. {OA}P passes, and {OA}O sorts your rack.

Commands
Type a step, as /R7-3.

Every game
{OA}R resigns, after asking first. {OA}T opens the chat. {ESC} goes back to the lobby, and the game goes on without you.

Rummy Tile help
The sets
A run is three or more tiles of one color in a row. A group is three or four tiles of one number in different colors. A joker stands for any tile.

The turn
Your first sets must be worth 30. A turn that does not count gives your tiles back and you take a tile. The first to empty the rack wins.

/say, /note, /time and /resign work at every table, and /tutorial shows how the games work.
