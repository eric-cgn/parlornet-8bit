Checkers help
Moving
Press {RET} on a piece to pick it up and on a marked square to move it there, or {ESC} to put it back. After the last step, end your turn.

Keys for Checkers
{OA}D offers a draw.
{OA}E ends your turn.
{OA}U takes back a step.

Commands
Type a step, as /11-15 or /15x22.

Every game
{OA}R resigns, after asking first. {OA}T opens the chat. {ESC} goes back to the lobby, and the game goes on without you.

Checkers help
The rules
Men move one square forward along the dark squares and kings move either way. A jump over a piece next to you takes it, and a jump that can go on must. A man that reaches the far row becomes a king.

The board
The dark squares are numbered 1 to 32. The player with no piece left, or no move, loses.

/say, /note, /time and /resign work at every table, and /tutorial shows how the games work.
