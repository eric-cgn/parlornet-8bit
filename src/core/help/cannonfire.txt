Cannon Fire help
Moving
Aim with the buttons and press {RET} on Fire. You may drive your tank once first.

Keys for Cannon Fire
< and > turn the barrel by 10.
+ and - change the power by 10.
[ and ] drive your tank left or right.

Commands
Type a shot after the slash, as /fire 45 60, or a drive, as /drive left 5.

Every game
{OA}R resigns, after asking first. {OA}T opens the chat. {ESC} goes back to the lobby, and the game goes on without you.

Cannon Fire help
The shot
The angle is 0 towards the right, 90 straight up and 180 towards the left. The power is 0 to 100, and the wind, when it blows, carries the shell.

The tanks
Each tank has its letter and its health over it, and a shell's burst takes health from the tanks near it. The last tank standing wins.

/say, /note, /time and /resign work at every table, and /tutorial shows how the games work.
