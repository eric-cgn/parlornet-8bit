Backgammon help
Moving
Press {RET} on Roll. Press {RET} on a checker to pick it up and on a marked point to move it, or {ESC} to put it back.

Keys for Backgammon
{OA}D doubles before you roll.
{OA}E ends your turn, and {OA}U takes back a step.
{OA}H moves your home board.

Commands
Type a step, as /13-9 or /6-off.

Every game
{OA}R resigns, after asking first. {OA}T opens the chat. {ESC} goes back to the lobby, and the game goes on without you.

Backgammon help
The board
Your checkers go round to your home board and off. A hit checker goes to the bar and comes back in first.

The cube
A double offers to play for twice as much. Accepting goes on with the cube, and declining loses the points on it.

Bearing off
With all your checkers home, a step can take one off.

/say, /note, /time and /resign work at every table, and /tutorial shows how the games work.
