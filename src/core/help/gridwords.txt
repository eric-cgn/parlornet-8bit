Grid Words help
Moving
Press {RET} where your word starts, and again to turn it down. Type the letters, and {RET} plays it. {ESC} takes the tiles back.

Keys for Grid Words
{OA}P passes, after asking first.
{OA}X starts an exchange.
{OA}V shows the points of every letter.

Commands
Type a play, as /h8 across QUIZ.

Every game
{OA}R resigns, after asking first. {OA}T opens the chat. {ESC} goes back to the lobby, and the game goes on without you.

Grid Words help
The squares
2L and 3L double or triple a letter, and 2W and 3W the whole word. The first word covers the middle square.

The tiles
A blank stands for any letter and scores nothing. A letter not on your rack uses a blank, if you have one.

/say, /note, /time and /resign work at every table, and /tutorial shows how the games work.
