Dots and Boxes help
Moving
Press {RET} on a dot, then on a dot next to it, to draw the line between them. {ESC} lets go of the first dot.

Keys for Dots and Boxes
Dots and Boxes has no keys of its own.

Commands
Type a line after the slash, as /a1-b1.

Every game
{OA}R resigns, after asking first. {OA}T opens the chat. {ESC} goes back to the lobby, and the game goes on without you.

Dots and Boxes help
The rules
Players take turns drawing a line between two dots next to each other. A line that closes a box wins it, with your letter in it, and you draw again. The player with the most boxes at the end wins.

The board
The columns of dots are a, b, c and on from the left, and the rows 1, 2, 3 and on from the top.

/say, /note, /time and /resign work at every table, and /tutorial shows how the games work.
