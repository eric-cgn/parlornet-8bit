Tutorial help
Moving
Move to a square with the arrows and press {RET} to place your X. The tutorial partner answers with an O.

Keys for the tutorial
{OA}U takes back your last X.

Commands
A move can be typed too, as /c3. /say says a line in the chat. Commands that need the parlor do nothing here.

Every game
{OA}R resigns, after asking first. {OA}T opens the chat. {ESC} goes back, and the game goes on without you.

Tutorial help
Three in a row
X and O take turns on a board of three by three squares. The first to have three in a row, across, down or corner to corner, wins.

The squares
The columns are a, b and c from the left, and the rows 1, 2 and 3 from the top, so the middle square is b2.

The tutorial is never played on the parlor. /tutorial starts it again any time.
