Lobby help
Your games, invitations and the tables waiting for a player.

Keys
The arrows move the pointer, and {RET} opens what it is on: a game of yours, an invitation's dialog or a table to join.
{OA}N opens a new table.
{OA}C goes to the line console.
{OA}Q signs out, after asking first.
/ opens the command line.

Commands
/who says who is online, and /top shows the best players.
/open GAME opens a table.
/tutorial shows how the games work.
/help lists every command.
