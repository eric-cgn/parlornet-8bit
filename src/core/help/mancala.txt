Mancala help
Moving
Your pits are the bottom row, 1 to 6 from the left, and your store is on the right. Move to a pit and press {RET} to sow its seeds.

Keys for Mancala
1 to 6 move the pointer to that pit.

Commands
Type a pit after the slash, as /3.

Every game
{OA}R resigns, after asking first. {OA}T opens the chat. {ESC} goes back to the lobby, and the game goes on without you.

The rules
Sowing drops the pit's seeds one by one in the pits after it. In Kalah your store takes one too, a last seed there gives you another turn, and a last seed in an empty pit of yours takes the seeds across. In Oware a last seed that makes two or three in the other row takes them.

The game ends when a row is empty, and the most seeds win.

/say, /note, /time and /resign work at every table, and /tutorial shows how the games work.
