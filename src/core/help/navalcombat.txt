Naval Combat help
Moving
Press {RET} on one end of a ship and then on its other end. Then press {RET} on the other grid to fire.

Keys for Naval Combat
{OA}G places the rest of your fleet at random.
{OA}U takes back a ship, and {OA}Z every ship.

Commands
Type a ship as /a1 across, or a shot as /e5.

Every game
{OA}R resigns, after asking first. {OA}T opens the chat. {ESC} goes back to the lobby, and the game goes on without you.

Naval Combat help
The fleet
The classic fleet has ships of 5, 4, 3, 3 and 2 squares, each placed across or down.

The shots
Each grid shows the hits and the misses, and a ship goes down when every square of it is hit. The first to sink the whole fleet wins.

/say, /note, /time and /resign work at every table, and /tutorial shows how the games work.
